# Calibration

Calibration maps image pixels to the graph's axis values. Every exported number depends on it, so the panel shows how well the clicks fit.

## Calibrating a graph

1. Press **C** (or choose the calibrate tool on the tool rail).
2. Click two labelled ticks on the X axis, then two on the Y axis. Use ticks far apart: the further apart, the more precise the result.
3. In the **Calibration** panel, type the value of each tick.
4. Set each axis scale to **Linear** or **Log₁₀**, and give each axis a label and unit if you like.

The badge changes to **Calibrated** once the four values are valid. The loupe and magnetic snap help place each click on the tick mark itself.

## Axes that do not cross at zero

Each axis group has a field saying where its ticks lie on the other axis (for example "X-axis ticks lie at Y ="). Leave it blank to solve it from the four clicks, or type it when you know it, such as when the ticks you clicked sit on an axis drawn at Y = 0.

## Photos taken at an angle

Turn on **Projective (photos taken at an angle)** for a photo or a skewed scan. The projective model corrects perspective; it needs the four clicks to be spread out across the graph.

## Changing or clearing a calibration

With the calibrate tool, drag a calibration marker to move it, or retype a value. **Clear calibration** removes it. Every change can be undone with Ctrl+Z.
