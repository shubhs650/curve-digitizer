# Getting started

Curve Digitizer turns a graph in a PDF or an image into numbers. Everything happens in your browser: files are never uploaded, and the app works offline once it has loaded. It works best in Chromium browsers such as Chrome and Edge.

The sidebar shows a **Getting started** checklist with the four steps below. Each step ticks itself when it is done, and the current step says what to do next. You can hide the checklist with its close button and bring it back with **Show the getting-started guide**.

## 1. Crop

Open a PDF with **Open PDF or image…** on the home screen, or drop the file anywhere on the window. Pick a page under **PDFs**, drag a rectangle around the graph and choose **Crop to new graph**. An image (PNG, JPEG, WebP) becomes a graph straight away; you can also paste one with Ctrl+V.

See [PDFs and images](pdf-and-images.md).

## 2. Calibrate

Press **C** for the calibrate tool. Click two labelled ticks on the X axis and two on the Y axis, and type the value of each tick in the **Calibration** panel.

See [Calibration](calibration.md).

## 3. Digitize

Press **P** for the add points tool and click along the curve. Points snap onto the line. Add a dataset for each further curve.

See [Digitizing points](digitizing.md) and [The data table](data-table.md).

## 4. Export

Use **Export** in the sidebar: CSV for the shown graph, or an Excel workbook with every graph.

See [Exporting](export.md).
